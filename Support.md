# Support — Today's Top 5

Thank you for using Today's Top 5! If you need help or have feedback, you're in the right place.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I add a task?
Tap the **+** button in the top-right corner of the Today tab. Type your task (up to 40 characters) and tap Save.

### Why can't I add more tasks?
You can have a maximum of **5 tasks per day**, including completed ones. This limit is intentional — it helps you focus on what matters most. Delete a completed task if you need to free up a slot.

### How do I reorder tasks?
Tap **Edit** in the top-left corner or long-press a task and drag it to a new position. Tasks are color-coded by priority: red (#1) through blue (#5).

### How do I delete a task?
Swipe left on any task (active or completed) to reveal the delete button.

### What happens to incomplete tasks at the end of the day?
When you open the App the next day, you'll be asked about each incomplete task:
- **Move to Today** — Carries the task to today's list
- **Drop** — Removes the task

### How do I change my reminder times?
Go to the **Settings** tab and adjust the planning and progress reminder times. Tap **Save Changes** to update.

### I'm not receiving notifications
1. Open **Settings** on your iPhone
2. Scroll down and tap **Today's Top 5**
3. Tap **Notifications**
4. Make sure **Allow Notifications** is turned on

If notifications were previously denied, the App will prompt you to enable them in Settings when you try to save reminder times.

### How do I see my progress?
Tap the **Analytics** tab to see a chart of your last 10 days, plus a summary card for today.

### How do I reset the App?
Delete and reinstall the App. This removes all locally stored data (tasks, settings, and analytics history).

### Does the App collect my data?
No. All your tasks and settings are stored locally on your device. We have no access to your data. The App uses Google AdMob for ads, which may collect device-level data as described in our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md).
