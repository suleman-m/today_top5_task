# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 6, 2026

**App Name:** Today's Top 5

This Privacy Policy describes how Today's Top 5 ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our iOS application.

## Information We Collect

### Data Stored Locally
The App stores the following data **locally on your device only**:

- **Tasks:** Task titles, completion status, dates, and priority order
- **Daily Records:** Aggregated daily statistics (number of tasks completed, added, moved)
- **Settings:** Your preferred reminder times and onboarding status

This data is stored using Apple's SwiftData framework and **never leaves your device**. We do not have access to your tasks or any personal information.

### Notifications
The App uses local notifications (not push notifications) to send you daily planning and progress reminders. Notification preferences are stored locally. No notification data is sent to any server.

### Advertising
The App uses **Google AdMob** to display banner advertisements. Google AdMob may collect certain data as described in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Information that Google AdMob may collect includes:
- Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID)
- IP address
- General location (derived from IP)
- App usage data (ad interactions)
- Device information (model, OS version)

Google may use this information to serve personalized or non-personalized ads depending on your device settings and consent. You can opt out of personalized advertising by:
1. Going to **Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking** on your iOS device
2. Disabling **"Allow Apps to Request to Track"**

For more information, see:
- [Google AdMob & Privacy](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## Information We Do Not Collect

We do **not** collect, store, or transmit:
- Your name, email, or contact information
- Account or login credentials (the App has no accounts)
- Location data
- Health data
- Financial data
- Photos, contacts, or other personal files
- Analytics or usage tracking beyond what Google AdMob collects

## Data Storage and Security

All App data (tasks, settings, daily records) is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Data is protected by your device's built-in security features, including:
- Device passcode / Face ID / Touch ID
- iOS data encryption

If you delete the App, all locally stored data is permanently removed.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Google AdMob is configured to comply with applicable regulations.

## Your Rights

Since all task data is stored locally on your device, you have full control:
- **Access:** View your data at any time within the App
- **Delete:** Delete individual tasks via swipe, or delete all data by removing the App
- **Portability:** Data is stored locally — no account to export from
